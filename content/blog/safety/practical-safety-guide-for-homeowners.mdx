Vetting a professional before they ever take a job is the foundation of safety on TaskLync, but a foundation is not the whole building. A few simple habits on your side of the booking make every job even safer, and none of them take more than a moment to follow.

## Verify Before the Door Opens

Every professional's profile shows their verified name, photo, trade category, and review history before you ever confirm a booking. When someone arrives at your door, take a few seconds to match their face to the photo on their profile. Their ID badge is also available right inside the app if you want to double check before letting them in.

This step exists because verification only protects you if it is actually checked at the moment it matters, not just trusted blindly because a booking was confirmed earlier in the day.

## Stay Present for Access Jobs

For jobs that involve access to multiple rooms or to utilities like water, gas, or electrical systems, it is worth staying home yourself or having a trusted adult present. If that is not possible, you can share your live location with a contact directly from the app for the duration of the booking, so someone else always knows exactly where you are and what is happening.

This is not about distrust of the professional you booked. It is a basic habit that costs nothing and adds a real layer of security to any job that involves someone moving through your home.

## If Something Feels Wrong, Stop the Job

You are never obligated to continue a job that does not feel right, for any reason. You can tap End Job in the app at any point, and you will not be charged for incomplete work. Our support team is available 24/7 through in app chat if you need help in the moment, not just after the fact.

Trust your own read of a situation. A professional passing every verification check does not mean every interaction will feel comfortable, and you should never feel like you have to push through discomfort just because a booking is already underway.

## Keep Payments in the App

Never pay cash before a job starts, and never transfer money to a professional outside the platform, no matter how the request is framed. TaskLync holds funds in escrow and only releases them once you confirm the work is actually complete. That structure exists specifically to protect you if a job goes wrong partway through.

If a professional asks you to pay outside the app, treat that as a signal on its own. It removes the protection the entire payment system is built to give you.

## The Bottom Line

TaskLync's vetting process is what keeps unqualified or unverified professionals from ever reaching your door in the first place. These four habits, checking who is actually standing in front of you, staying present or sharing your location for bigger jobs, stopping a job the moment something feels wrong, and keeping every payment inside the app, are what carry that same protection through the entire booking, not just the moment before it starts.
